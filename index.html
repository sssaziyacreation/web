The spirit of Saziya
Every garment carries the care of the hands that made it.

S S Saziya Creation began in Surat in 2013 with one belief from our founder, Wasihaidar Ansari: every garment deserves workmanship, and every client’s vision deserves a proper conversation. Today we make AS3 men’s ethnic wear, a full ladies’ range from plain to export level, and custom and private label collections for other businesses.

AS3 men’s ethnic wear, redefined

AS3 is our main brand, built for men who like their tradition worn with confidence. Kurta, kurta-pajama, thobe and jubbah collections are made for everyday wear, festive occasions and everything between.

Ladies’ wear from plain to premium

Our ladies’ range covers kurtis, coordinated sets, pants, palazzos and dupattas. Choose plain essentials for volume, premium detailing for occasion wear, or export-level quality for overseas markets.

Why choose S S Saziya Creation

We are a Surat manufacturer that starts with a real conversation. Fabric, fit, construction and delivery are agreed with you before an order is confirmed, so the final garment matches the brief.

Wholesale and retail for AS3

Traders and shops can enquire for bulk size assortments, while individual buyers can ask for smaller retail quantities. Both routes come with direct answers from our team.

Custom and private label manufacturing

Bring a reference, sketch or brand idea. We help develop the garment, sample it and manufacture it under your own label, for boutiques, brands and other businesses.

Fabric, fit and finish

Good garments are decided by details: fabric feel, clean stitching, accurate measurements and careful finishing. Each order is reviewed against the agreed specifications before packing.

Made in Surat, open to the world

Surat is one of India’s best-known textile cities, and it is our home since 2013. We welcome enquiries from across India and overseas, with sampling, capacity and delivery confirmed individually.

Work for every wardrobe

From simple daily wear to distinctive occasion styles, our collections are designed so retailers and brands can build a balanced, versatile range with one manufacturing partner.

Frequently asked questions

Answers before you ask.

What does AS3 offer?

AS3 is our men’s ethnic wear brand, with kurta, kurta-pajama and thobe / jubbah collections. It is available through wholesale and retail enquiries.

Can I buy AS3 in small quantities?

Yes. Retail enquiries are welcome for smaller quantities. Share the style and size you need and we will confirm availability.

What quality levels do you make in ladies’ wear?

We work across plain everyday garments, premium finished styles and export-level production. The right tier depends on your market and budget.

Do you accept custom and private label orders?

Yes. Share your design reference, fabric preference and quantity, and we will discuss construction, sampling and production before confirming.

What are your minimum quantities and timelines?

These depend on the category and design. Order acceptance, sampling, capacity and delivery are confirmed individually on enquiry.

Do you work with overseas buyers?

Yes. We welcome overseas enquiries. Documentation and delivery arrangements are confirmed individually for each order.
